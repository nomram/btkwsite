Alignment app

Drag the orange dots onto the parts, and the dark squares to where the name should be written

The line goes from the dot to the square, the name sits on a short rule that starts at the square (direction: to the right or to the left). The square may be outside the picture. On the site the line and the name only show while the pointer is on the dot.

Dots

Move, scale and rotate the mirrored cutaway over the ZMP 610 (left pump)

Drag the picture to move it. Mouse wheel scales it, Shift + wheel rotates it, arrow keys nudge it (Shift = 5 px), Q / E rotate by 0.1°. The sliders on the right set the exact numbers: scale across and up/down separately, rotation, and skew. Match the pressure gauge, the outlet on the right, the ball valves and the inlet at the bottom.

Transform

Rotation and skew turn around the middle of the cutaway. Skew across leans the top against the bottom, skew up/down leans the left against the right.
