Alignment app

Drag the orange dots onto the parts, and the dark squares to where the label should be

The line goes from the dot to the square. A name is written on a short rule that starts at the square; a picture sits on the square with its corner (up/down and left/right as chosen). The square may be outside the picture. On the site the line and the label only show while the pointer is on the dot.

Dots

Placement and zoom of the card photos on the products page

Drag the photo to choose which part stays in view, mouse wheel (or the slider) to zoom. The frame has the same proportions (3:2) as on the site; the thin lines are thirds. Click another photo below to adjust it. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Move, scale and rotate the mirrored cutaway over the ZMP 610 (left pump)

Drag the picture to move it. Mouse wheel scales it, Shift + wheel rotates it, arrow keys nudge it (Shift = 5 px), Q / E rotate by 0.1°. The sliders on the right set the exact numbers: scale across and up/down separately, rotation, and skew. Match the pressure gauge, the outlet on the right, the ball valves and the inlet at the bottom.

Transform

Rotation and skew turn around the middle of the cutaway. Skew across leans the top against the bottom, skew up/down leans the left against the right.
